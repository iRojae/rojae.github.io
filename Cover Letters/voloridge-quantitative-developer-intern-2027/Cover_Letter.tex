I am writing to apply for the Quantitative Developer Intern 2027 position at Voloridge Investment Management. I also completed J.P. Morgan's Quantitative Research Forage simulation, covering statistical analysis, derivatives pricing, and credit-risk modeling. These projects reflect the curiosity, problem-solving mindset, and strong mathematical foundation I would bring to Voloridge---and my eagerness to learn your technology stacks, development standards, and best practices in quantitative investment.

I am highly motivated, detail-oriented, and comfortable working in team settings where clear communication matters. I am fully available to work onsite in Jupiter for Summer 2027 and welcome the opportunity to relocate for the internship. Thank you for your time and consideration. I would welcome the chance to discuss how my background in computational data analysis and quantitative modeling can support Voloridge's development team.
