# Call Prep Kit

Use this for every 15-minute alumni conversation.

---

## Before the call (5 min prep)

1. Re-read their LinkedIn — note firm, role, SBU degree, one recent post or project
2. Pick **3 questions** from the bank below (one exploration, one tactical, one referral)
3. Have open: your site, GitHub repo, one sentence pitch memorized
4. Test Zoom/phone link

**Your one-sentence pitch:**
> I've spent undergrad doing rigorous physics research with large datasets and simulation pipelines at BNL; I'm now building ML projects to see how those skills transfer to market microstructure and systematic research.

---

## 15-minute call structure

| Time | What to do |
|------|------------|
| 0–2 min | Thank them. One sentence on you (physics, BNL, exploring quant, early-stage LOB project). |
| 2–8 min | **Listen.** Ask about their SBU → quant path. Take 2–3 bullet notes. |
| 8–12 min | Ask your 2 prepared questions. |
| 12–14 min | "Is there anyone else you'd recommend I speak with?" |
| 14–15 min | Thank them. Ask permission to follow up in a few weeks with project progress. |

---

## Questions by goal

### Exploration (pick 1)
- What does a typical week look like in your role?
- What surprised you most coming from [physics/math] into quant?
- How much of your work is research vs engineering vs production?

### Internship / recruiting (pick 1)
- When did you start preparing for quant interviews?
- What firms or teams would you recommend for someone with a physics/simulation background?
- What did your first internship interview loop look like?

### Full-time / positioning (pick 1)
- How did you position physics research on your resume?
- If you were in my position a year before recruiting, what would you focus on?
- What skills from SBU mattered most in your first year?

### Referral (always ask at end)
- Is there anyone else you'd recommend I speak with?

## Thank-you note (send within 24 hours)

**Template:**

```
Hi [Name],

Thank you again for taking the time to speak with me today. I especially appreciated your point about [ONE SPECIFIC THING THEY SAID — e.g. "starting probability prep early" or "framing physics research as pipeline + data work on a resume"].

I'll keep building my LOB project and will follow up in a few weeks with progress. Thanks again for the guidance.

Best,
Rojae
```

**Do NOT** ask for a referral in the thank-you note.
